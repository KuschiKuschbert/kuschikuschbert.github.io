Rocktally Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026

The short version

Rocktally keeps your counts on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet at all. We never see your data.

Who we are

Rocktally is an Android tally counter made by Daniel Kuschmierz, an independent developer in Rockhampton, Queensland, Australia. In this policy "we" means the developer and "the app" means Rocktally.

What the app stores

Everything below is saved only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your counters: their names, counts, targets and milestones, and the groups you put them in (group names).
  • Press history: the time of each change, whether it went up or down, where it came from (volume key, tap, widget, notification) and the count afterwards.
  • Your settings, such as theme, accent colour, sleep time and vibration choices.
  • A short diagnostics log of counting events, kept in memory only and cleared when the app closes.

The app doesn't request the internet permission, so none of this can be sent anywhere by the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't collect, receive, sell or share any personal or usage data. There are no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools in the app.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose:

  • CSV export. When you export history, you pick where the file is saved or shared. What happens to it after that is up to you and the app or service you choose.
  • Diagnostics. If you copy or share the diagnostics log from the hidden diagnostics screen, it goes where you send it.
  • Android backup. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Rocktally's counters, settings and history in your own Google backup or a phone-to-phone transfer. That backup belongs to your Google account; we can't access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Permissions and why they're needed

PermissionWhy
NotificationsShows the live count while counting, with buttons to take one off, switch counters or stop.
Foreground serviceKeeps counting your volume key presses while the screen is off or another app is open. You start it, and it stops itself after the quiet time you set.
VibrationGives a buzz for each press, milestones and targets, so you can count without looking.
Display over other appsOptional and off by default. Shows a small count pill over other apps if you turn it on.

Deleting your data

Delete a counter in the app to remove it and its history, or uninstall Rocktally to remove everything it stored. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings does the same.

Children

Rocktally is a general-audience utility. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
