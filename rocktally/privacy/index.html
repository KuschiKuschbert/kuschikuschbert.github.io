Rocktally Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026

The short version

Rocktally keeps your counts on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet at all. We never see your data, unless you choose to send us something, like an email or a diagnostics report.

Who we are

Rocktally is an Android tally counter made by Daniel Kuschmierz, an independent developer in Rockhampton, Queensland, Australia. In this policy "we" means the developer and "the app" means Rocktally.

What the app stores

Everything below is saved only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your counters: their names, counts, targets (and whether a target starts new rounds or is a limit) and milestones, and the groups you put them in (group names).
  • Press history: the time of each change, whether it went up or down, where it came from (volume key, tap, widget, notification) and the count afterwards.
  • Your settings, such as theme, accent colour, sleep time and vibration choices.
  • A short diagnostics log of counting events, kept in memory only and cleared when the app closes.
  • When you restore a backup file: a copy of what was on the phone just before, so the restore can be undone.

The app doesn't request the internet permission, so none of this can be sent anywhere by the app.

What we collect

Nothing. The app doesn't collect, send, sell or share any personal or usage data, and there are no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools in it.

The only way anything reaches us is if you choose to send it, for example an email or a diagnostics report. See If you email us or send a diagnostics report.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose:

  • CSV export. When you export history, you pick where the file is saved or shared. What happens to it after that is up to you and the app or service you choose.
  • Backup file. When you save a backup, you pick where it goes. The app suggests your phone's Documents folder first. If you choose an online folder such as Google Drive, that copy is kept by that service under your account; we can't see it or delete it, and anyone you share it with can see your counter names and history.
  • Autosave. If you turn it on (the how-to offers it), the app writes the same backup file each day you count to Documents/Rocktally on your phone, or a folder you pick, and keeps the last 7. Files in Documents stay after you uninstall the app, and other apps you allow to read your files can read them. An online folder you pick works as described above.
  • Diagnostics report. The hidden diagnostics screen can build a report to help fix counting problems. Before you copy or share it, the app shows what's in it and asks you to agree. It goes only where you paste or send it.
  • Android backup. If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Rocktally's counters, settings and history in your own Google backup or a phone-to-phone transfer. That backup belongs to your Google account; we can't access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

If you email us or send a diagnostics report

A diagnostics report contains:

  • The app version and whether it came from Google Play.
  • Your phone's model, Android version and security patch.
  • Screen size, font size, language and clock format.
  • Rocktally's battery, notification and background settings on your phone.
  • The type of audio output (speaker, wired or Bluetooth) and the music volume.
  • Rocktally's own settings, its counting state, how it last closed (for example a crash, with times), the results of the guided test and the diagnostics log (times and counting events only).

It doesn't contain your counts, your counter or group names, your history, contacts, location or any account. The name of the counter in use and the name of a Bluetooth device are left out unless you switch them on before sharing.

If you send a report or an email to us:

  • We use it only to answer you and fix the problem. We don't sell or share it, use it for marketing or combine it with other data.
  • We delete the report when we're done with the problem. Ask us any time and we'll delete it, and your emails, sooner.
  • Emails and reports arrive in our Gmail inbox, which Google hosts.

Permissions and why they're needed

PermissionWhy
NotificationsShows the live count while counting, with buttons to take one off, switch counters or stop.
Foreground serviceKeeps counting your volume key presses while the screen is off or another app is open. You start it, and it stops itself after the quiet time you set.
Live updates (Android 16 and newer)Lets the counting notification show the count and progress in the status bar while counting is awake. No prompt; you can turn it off in Rocktally's settings or Android's.
VibrationGives a buzz for each press, milestones, targets and limits, so you can count without looking.
Display over other appsOptional and off by default. Shows a small count pill over other apps if you turn it on.

Deleting your data

Delete a counter in the app to remove it and its history, or uninstall Rocktally to remove everything it stored. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings does the same.

Children

Rocktally is a general-audience utility. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
