Rocktally

A tally counter you use without looking. Volume Up adds one, Volume Down takes one off, with the screen off and your music still playing.

What people count with it

For prayer and dhikr

Count dhikr with the volume button, eyes closed and screen off. Set 33 or 100, feel the treasure buzz when a round is done, then carry on in a new colour.

For doors and venues

Volume Up for in, Volume Down for out. Set a Limit for your capacity: see how many are in now and how many came in today, and it turns red with a stronger buzz when you're over.

For sport, crafts and work

Laps, reps and sets without unlocking your phone, rows and stitches, kids onto the bus, birds, stocktake: anything you count.

Everything else

  • As many named counters as you like, free, in groups with a total per group
  • Targets with rounds, or a Limit for capacity
  • Set a count by hand, hold the number to reset, Undo for everything
  • Live count on your lock screen, a Quick Settings tile and two home screen widgets
  • History by day and hour, CSV export, backup files and a daily autosave
  • In 9 languages, light or dark, on phones, foldables and flip phones
No ads. No subscription. No account. No internet.

Rocktally never connects to the internet, so your counts never leave your phone.
